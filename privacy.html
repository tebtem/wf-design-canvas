Privacy Notice

Last updated November 13, 2026

In short: the canvas you build on your own stays in your browser. We never see it. If you join or host a workshop, we store what is needed to run it, and it is deleted within 30 days. We do not sell your information or show ads.

Who we are

The Facilitation Design Canvas™ is provided by Women Facilitating. You can reach us through womenfacilitating.com.

Using the canvas on your own

  • Everything you type is saved only in your web browser on your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • If you clear your browser data or use a private window, your canvas can be lost. Download a backup to keep a copy. Backup files are saved on your device, and only you decide where they go.
  • Printing or saving as PDF happens in your browser.

Joining a workshop

When you join a workshop with a code, we store, for that workshop only:

  • the name you enter,
  • your workshop canvas and the comments your facilitator sends you,
  • a random rejoin code and an anonymous sign-in ID, so you can return to your canvas,
  • whether you are currently connected, so the facilitator can see who is online.

Your facilitators can see your name and canvas. Other participants can see them only if the facilitator turns on sharing or spotlights your canvas. Facilitator comments are visible only to you and the facilitators.

Hosting a workshop

Facilitators sign in with their email address using a one-time link. We use the email address only to sign you in and to confirm you are allowed to host.

How long we keep workshop information

Workshop information is deleted as soon as the facilitator ends the workshop, and automatically no more than 30 days after the workshop was created. A copy of your own canvas also stays in your browser until you remove it.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores workshop information and handles sign-in. It acts on our behalf as a data processor.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.
  • Umami, if enabled, counts visits and a few actions (for example, saving a PDF) so we can improve the canvas. It does not use cookies, does not collect what you type, and does not identify you.

Email updates

If you choose to sign up for emails from us, we use MailerLite to send them. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. Signing up is always optional and is never required to use the canvas.

Cookies

The canvas does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's storage to save your canvas, remember your layout choice, and keep you signed in to a workshop.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used. For workshop information, you can ask your facilitator to end the workshop, or contact us through womenfacilitating.com. Your solo canvas is in your own browser, and you can delete it anytime with Clear canvas.

Children

The canvas is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted on the canvas.
